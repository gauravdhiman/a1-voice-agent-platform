# Opportunity in motion
A bespoke film system, grounded in the public site's day/night tokens and official cobalt phone mark. Large clean type and a purposeful signal are the visual identity. No decorative AI orb or copied reference palette.

Display ranges from 96 to 230 px at 1080p. Primary content stays above 900 px. The small bottom rail is chapter/provenance, not a subtitle band. Camera motion has a destination and settles. Fine lines form readable cause-and-effect diagrams. Perspective belongs to the phone and product surfaces; typography remains crisp.
