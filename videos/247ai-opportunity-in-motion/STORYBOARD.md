# Opportunity in motion

This video tells local business owners that customers still get answers, help and appointments when the business closes, while the owner focuses on their work.

## Video direction
Brand day/night surfaces, cobalt for energy and mint for completed action. A circular call signal carries the film. Long-tail motion, tightly paced reveals, motivated perspective. Deliberate held reads at the booked result, review state and final URL. No feature-slide repetition, drifting card wallpaper, unsupported metrics or reference imitation. Every scene is an editable sub-composition. Workflow illustrations are labeled.

| Frame | Time | On screen | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 — The moment | 00–08 | “Doors close. AI still answers.” A dimensional phone shows an after-hours caller, with an answered-call label. | Makes the after-hours promise explicit |
| 02 — The answer | 08–16 | Ring expands through frame; official mark and 24x7 AI resolve; “Calls answered. Appointments booked.” | Lands the core customer outcomes |
| 03 — Everywhere | 16–24 | Phone on the left, real public site crop on the right, signal bridges them into a voice waveform | Shows the two customer entry points |
| 04 — Your business | 24–32 | Oversized hours/services/workflows token cycle, organized orbital labels feeding the voice | Explains relevant, business-specific answers |
| 05 — Booked | 32–40 | Customer question, availability scan, a calendar tile turns mint and confirms | Proves conversation → action |
| 06 — Keep it moving | 40–48 | Captured request travels along a drawn line into calendar/email/records nodes | Makes integration valuable |
| 07 — In your control | 48–56 | Plain-language instruction; test waveform; review recording trace, revealed in order | Establishes owner control |
| 08 — Room for your work | 56–64 | Typography expands “Less interruption.” → “More possibility.” Signal draws a widening open frame | Emotional payoff |
| 09 — Hear it | 64–72 | Signal and official phone mark resolve, 24x7 AI, “Your after-hours AI front desk.”, “Hear what happens next.” and 247ai.live | One clear invitation |
