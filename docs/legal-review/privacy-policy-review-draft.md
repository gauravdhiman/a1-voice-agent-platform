This Privacy Policy explains how 24x7 AI collects, uses, stores, and shares personal information through its business platform and website. It covers account users and people who communicate with an organization through its configured agents or workflows. An organization may also have its own privacy notice for callers and employees; this Policy does not replace that notice.

## 1. Information we handle

Depending on how the Service is used, we may handle:

- **Account and organization information**, such as names, work contact details, sign-in identifiers, organization settings, user roles, invitations, and records of policy acceptance.
- **Customer content and configuration**, such as agent instructions, settings, files, contact details, and information an organization supplies through a connected service or workflow.
- **Communication information**, such as phone numbers or other contact details, call timing and duration, call status, routing and usage details, and voice or text content needed to conduct a conversation or complete an action.
- **Billing and support information**, such as plan and invoice status, payment processor references, support messages, and details needed to investigate an issue. Payments are handled by a payment provider; 24x7 AI does not receive full card numbers through the application.
- **Device, usage, and security information**, such as browser and device details, page or feature usage, diagnostic events, and security logs. Optional product analytics may run when enabled for a deployment.

We receive this information from account users and organizations, people who contact or speak with an organization’s agent, connected services the organization authorizes, payment and communication providers, and the devices and services used to access the platform.

## 2. How we use information

We use information to provide and operate the Service; authenticate users; route communications; generate AI responses; perform organization-selected actions; administer accounts and billing; send configured service notifications; provide support; protect the Service; investigate misuse; and meet legal obligations. We may use aggregated information that no longer identifies a person to understand service performance and improve reliability.

We do not use customer or call information to train general-purpose AI models operated by 24x7 AI, or to target advertising. Information may be sent to an AI, speech, telephony, or other service provider when that is needed to provide a feature configured by an organization. Provider data practices can depend on the service and account settings. We will update our provider disclosures when a provider or a change in its data use materially affects the information handled through the Service; providers may have different retention and model-improvement practices.

## 3. Voice conversations and recordings

When an organization uses voice features, audio and related information are processed to connect the conversation and generate a response. The selected providers may process voice audio, text, and technical call information. A provider may generate transcription in real time as part of the conversation. The organization configuring the agent is responsible for deciding whether the use is appropriate, informing callers, and obtaining any consent required by law.

Depending on the organization’s configuration and the features enabled for its account, a voice interaction may be recorded and stored. Recordings are retained under the Service’s current retention schedule and can be deleted earlier by an organization administrator with the required permission. Deleting a recording removes its audio object; the related call log and operational or billing metadata may remain. We retain other information for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described here, including account operation, security, billing, legal obligations, and dispute resolution.

## 4. How information is shared

We share information with service providers that help us operate the Service, including providers for:

- cloud hosting, databases, authentication, network delivery, and storage;
- AI, speech recognition, speech generation, and voice processing;
- telephone and communication delivery;
- payment processing, email, and administrator notifications;
- optional product analytics and operational security; and
- integrations or tools that an organization chooses to connect.

These providers receive information needed to perform their services. Organizations may also direct information to their own connected services, such as calendar, email, spreadsheet, CRM, webhook, or other business systems. When a workflow reads information from a connected service and uses it to generate an AI response or complete an action, that information may be processed by the providers needed for that workflow.

We may disclose information to comply with law, protect people or the Service, enforce our agreements, or complete a business transaction subject to appropriate protections. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, as those terms are defined by applicable law, and we do not use call content for targeted advertising.

A person who spoke with an organization’s agent may need to contact that organization to exercise rights over information the organization controls.

Depending on the law that applies, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to object to certain uses. Send a request to [ONE MONITORED COMPANY EMAIL ADDRESS]. We will verify and respond to requests as required by applicable law. Some information may be retained when required by law or needed for security, billing, dispute resolution, or the operation of the Service.

## 6. Roles and responsibilities

An organization generally decides why caller or employee information is collected and how it is used. For that information, 24x7 AI generally processes data to provide the organization’s configured Service. We separately use account, billing, security, and service-administration information to operate our business. The legal roles and any additional contract required depend on the customer and the applicable law.

## 7. Security and international processing

We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. No online service can guarantee that transmission or storage is completely secure. Our service providers may process information in the countries where they or their infrastructure operate. Where applicable law requires safeguards for international transfers, we use the safeguards required for those transfers.

## 8. Children

The Service is designed for business use, and account holders must be adults. Some features and providers may restrict use for certain audiences or activities, including services likely to be accessed by children or clinical and medical use. Organizations may configure those features only for eligible audiences and use cases. We do not knowingly design the account service to collect information directly from children.
