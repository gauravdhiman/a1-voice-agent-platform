**Website:** https://247ai.live
**Privacy requests and questions:** privacy@247ai.live
**Mailing address:** Cognitive Entities LLC, Peoria, Arizona, USA. Our registered address is on file with the Arizona Corporation Commission (Business ID 25135926). We have not appointed a data protection officer; contact us at the email above.

This Privacy Policy explains how 24x7 AI collects, uses, stores, and shares personal information through its business platform, website, and live demos. It covers three groups differently, and Section 6 explains those roles:

- **Account users** — people who work at a business customer and use our platform.
- **Callers and website visitors who talk to an agent** — people who communicate with a business through its configured 24x7 AI agent, or who try a live demo on our website.
- **Website visitors** — people who browse 247ai.live.

A business customer may also have its own privacy notice for its callers and employees; this Policy does not replace that notice.

## 1. Information we handle

Depending on how the Service is used, we may handle:

- **Account and organization information**, such as names, work contact details, sign-in identifiers, organization settings, user roles, invitations, and records of policy acceptance.
- **Customer content and configuration**, such as agent instructions and settings, business information the agent uses (hours, prices, services), files, contact details, and information an organization supplies through a connected service or workflow.
- **Communication information**, such as phone numbers or other contact details, call timing and duration, call status, routing and usage details, call recordings and transcripts where recording is enabled, and voice or text content needed to conduct a conversation or complete an action (for example booking an appointment or capturing a callback request). The same applies to conversations with our website voice demos.
- **Billing and support information**, such as plan and invoice status, payment processor references, support messages, and details needed to investigate an issue. Payments are handled by our payment provider (Stripe); we do not receive or store full card numbers.
- **Device, usage, and security information**, such as browser and device details, IP address, page or feature usage, diagnostic events, and security logs. See Section 8 for cookies and analytics.

We receive this information from account users and organizations; from people who contact or speak with an organization's agent or our demos; from connected services the organization authorizes; from payment and communication providers; and from the devices and services used to access the platform.

## 2. How we use information

We use information to: provide and operate the Service; authenticate users; connect and route communications; generate AI responses; perform organization-selected actions (such as booking, notifications, and record-keeping); administer accounts and billing; send configured service notifications; provide support; protect the Service and prevent misuse; comply with law; and enforce our agreements. We may use aggregated or de-identified information that no longer identifies a person to understand service performance and improve reliability.

**No AI training on your data.** We do not use customer content, call recordings, transcripts, or caller information to train general-purpose AI models operated by 24x7 AI, and we do not use them to target advertising.

**Subprocessor training — an important limit.** Information is sent to AI, speech, and telephony providers when needed to provide a feature an organization configured. All customer production traffic — including pilot accounts and the public demos on our website — runs on provider configurations and paid contract tiers that do not permit the provider to train its general models on data submitted through them, and we do not route production traffic through free or trial provider tiers whose terms allow such use. Any free-tier provider keys we hold are used only for internal testing with our own test data, never for customer or public traffic. **Exception — an organization's own provider accounts:** where an organization connects its own provider account or API keys, that provider's use of the data it processes is governed by the organization's own agreement with that provider (including any training or retention terms in that agreement), which the organization is responsible for reviewing; the commitment in this paragraph applies to provider accounts and configurations we manage. If a provider we manage changes its terms in a way that materially affects this commitment, we will update Section 4 and this Policy and, where required, notify customers before the change applies to their traffic.

**No automated decisions with legal effect.** Our agents carry out tasks a business configured (answering questions, booking, capturing requests). We do not use personal information to make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about a person solely by automated means.

## 3. Voice conversations, recordings, and biometrics

When an organization uses voice features, audio and related information are processed to connect the conversation and generate a response. Providers may process voice audio, text, and technical call information, and may generate a transcription in real time as part of the conversation.

**Recording.** Depending on the organization's configuration and the features enabled for its account, a voice interaction may be recorded and stored. **Our default retention for call recordings is 90 days**, after which the audio is automatically deleted. An organization administrator with the required permission can delete a recording earlier. Deleting a recording removes its audio file; the related call log and operational or billing metadata may remain for the periods in Section 7. The organization configuring the agent is responsible for deciding whether recording is appropriate, informing callers that they are speaking with an AI agent and that the call may be recorded, and obtaining any consent required by law (some states and countries require every party's consent).

**No voiceprints.** We do not create voiceprints or other biometric identifiers, and we do not use voice audio to identify a speaker by their voice. Voice is processed to understand and respond to what is said, and to produce a recording and transcript where the organization has enabled them.

## 4. How information is shared — our service providers

We share information with service providers ("subprocessors") that help us operate the Service. They receive only the information needed to perform their services and are bound by contract or their business terms to use it for that purpose. Depending on the features and configuration an organization uses, our providers include:

- **Cloud hosting, database, authentication, and storage:** Google Cloud, Supabase, Oracle Cloud Infrastructure, Cloudflare (including Cloudflare R2 storage)
- **Telephony and communications delivery:** Twilio
- **AI models, speech recognition, and speech generation:** Google (Gemini), OpenAI, Deepgram, Cartesia, Fish Audio, Cerebras, and OpenRouter (which routes to the model provider selected in the configuration), used depending on the agent's configuration
- **Payment processing:** Stripe
- **Email and service notifications:** Resend, Telegram (only where an organization configures Telegram notifications)
- **Product analytics (optional, where enabled for a deployment):** PostHog

**Organization-directed sharing.** Organizations may direct information to their own connected services, such as calendar, email, spreadsheet, CRM, webhook, or other business systems (for example Google Calendar, Gmail, Google Sheets, and HubSpot). When a workflow reads information from a connected service and uses it to generate an AI response or complete an action, that information may be processed by the providers needed for that workflow. The organization controls those connections and can disconnect them.

**Other disclosures.** We may disclose information to comply with law or a valid legal request; to protect people, our customers, or the Service; to investigate fraud or misuse; to enforce our agreements; or as part of a merger, sale, or other business transaction, subject to appropriate protections and notice where required by law.

**No sale of personal information.** We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, as those terms are defined under applicable law (including the California Consumer Privacy Act), and we do not use call content for targeted advertising.

## 5. Google user data — Limited Use disclosure

Where an organization connects a Google account (for example Google Calendar, Gmail, or Google Sheets), our use of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. In particular: we use Google user data only to provide and improve the user-facing features the organization configured; we do not transfer it except as necessary to provide those features, to comply with law, or as part of a business transaction with consent; we do not use it for advertising; and we do not allow humans to read it except with the account holder's consent, for security or legal reasons, or in aggregated and anonymized form for internal operations. Connecting an organization always authorizes only the scopes shown on Google's consent screen.

## 6. Roles and responsibilities

An organization generally decides why caller or employee information is collected and how it is used. For that information, the organization is the **controller** (or "business") and 24x7 AI is its **processor** (or "service provider/contractor"): we process that information only on the organization's documented instructions as configured in the Service, to provide the Service, and as required by law. We will assist the organization, on request and where the law requires, with privacy requests from its callers relating to that information.

We act as controller/business for account, billing, security, website, and service-administration information that we use to operate our own business.

## 7. How long we keep information

We keep information only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes in this Policy:

- **Call recordings:** 90 days by default, then the audio is automatically deleted; an administrator may delete sooner, and an organization may set a shorter period where the Service offers that setting.
- **Call logs, transcripts, and conversation records:** kept while the account is active so the organization can review and use them; deleted or de-identified within 90 days after account closure, except as below.
- **Account, organization, and configuration data:** kept while the account is active; deleted or de-identified within 90 days after account closure, except as below.
- **Billing and transaction records:** kept for up to 7 years for tax, accounting, and legal compliance.
- **Security and diagnostic logs:** kept for up to 12 months.
- **Support communications:** kept for up to 3 years.

Some information may be retained longer where required by law, to resolve disputes, to enforce agreements, or to protect the Service. Backup copies are overwritten in the normal backup cycle.

## 8. Cookies and analytics on our website

Our platform uses cookies and similar technologies that are **necessary** for sign-in, security, and core functionality; these cannot be switched off in our systems. Where enabled for a deployment, we also use **optional analytics** (PostHog) to understand page and feature usage. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies, and we do not sell information collected through cookies. You can control cookies through your browser settings; blocking necessary cookies will break sign-in. We honor a browser's Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal as an opt-out of any sale or sharing of personal information, to the extent applicable — although, as stated in Section 4, we do not sell or share personal information for advertising in the first place. Our website is directed at businesses in the United States; if you access it from a region whose law requires consent before optional analytics, contact us and we will disable optional analytics for your account.

Organization administrators can manage account access, disconnect available integrations, change agent configuration, and delete recordings where they have the required permission. A person who spoke with an organization's agent should contact that organization first to exercise rights over information the organization controls; we will support the organization in responding.

Depending on the law that applies to you (for example California and other US states with privacy laws, and, where applicable, laws outside the US), you may have rights to: know and access the personal information held about you; request correction of inaccurate information; request deletion; obtain a portable copy; opt out of sale or sharing (we do not sell or share, as stated above); limit certain uses of sensitive information; and not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. You may also use an authorized agent where the law allows.

To make a request about information **we** control, contact privacy@247ai.live. We will verify your identity proportionately to the request and respond within the time required by applicable law (generally within 45 days under California law, extendable once where permitted, with notice). Some information may be retained when required by law or needed for security, billing, dispute resolution, or operation of the Service, and we will explain if we cannot fully comply. If you disagree with our response, you may appeal by replying to our decision email; we will respond as required by applicable law, and California residents may contact the California Attorney General.

**Security incidents.** If a security incident involving personal information triggers a notification duty under applicable law, we will notify affected customers and, where required, individuals and regulators, without unreasonable delay and as required by those laws, and we will tell customer organizations promptly so they can meet their own duties to their callers.
