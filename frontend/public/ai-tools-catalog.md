- Pricing: From $12/mo (Lite); 60-day free trial, no free plan
- Best for: Nonprofits, local businesses, and event organizers who want the simplest possible email tool with human support
- Verdict: Constant Contact is the friendliest email tool for non-marketers, and its phone support plus event features are genuinely distinctive. You pay a premium for that friendliness, and power users will outgrow it.
- Strengths: The easiest email builder in the category: genuinely learnable in under an hour, even for non-technical owners; Phone support on every paid plan, which is rare at this price and a lifeline when something breaks before a big send; A 60-day free trial with no credit card, long enough to actually learn the tool before paying
- Watch out: No free plan since June 2025, while Mailchimp and most rivals offer one; Real automation is gated: Lite gets one automation template, and meaningful automation needs Standard or Premium
