You have probably heard the term "AI receptionist" a dozen times by now,
usually from someone trying to sell you one. Here is the no-pitch version:
what it is, what it is not, and the six questions I hear from every business
owner who asks about it.

## What is an AI receptionist?

It is software that answers your business phone calls and has real
conversations with callers. Not a press-1 phone tree. A voice that greets the
caller, answers questions about your hours and prices, books appointments into
your calendar, takes orders, and writes down callback requests with the
caller's name, number, and reason.

Think of it as the world's most reliable front-desk employee: it never sleeps,
never puts anyone on hold, and handles three calls at once without breaking a
sweat.

## Does it sound like a robot?

This is the first question everyone asks, and it is fair. Early versions did
sound robotic. The current ones do not. Callers hear a natural voice that
pauses, confirms details, and handles interruptions the way a person would.

Here is my honest take: a small number of callers will guess it is AI. Almost
none of them care, because their problem got solved in thirty seconds. People
do not call your business hoping for small talk. They call because the AC is
out or they need a table for Friday.

## What happens when someone calls?

The AI answers in about four seconds on your existing business number. It
greets the caller the way you trained it to, figures out what they need, and
acts: booking, answering, ordering, or capturing a callback. You get a
notification with the outcome seconds later, and everything is logged: who
called, what they wanted, what happened.

## What if the caller wants a real human?

Then the smartest thing the AI can do is get out of the way. It takes their
details and flags the request to you instantly, with context, so your callback
starts warm instead of cold. "Hi, this is Maria, you called about the deep
cleaning price" beats "uh, someone called?" every time.

## How is this different from voicemail or an answering service?

Voicemail is where customers go to disappear. Remember the stat: up to 85% of
callers who hit voicemail never call back. Traditional answering services are
better, but they take messages and relay them later. An AI receptionist
resolves the call: the appointment is booked, the question is answered, the
order is taken. Resolution beats relay.
