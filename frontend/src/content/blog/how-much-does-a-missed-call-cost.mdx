I will keep this short, because the math does the talking.

CallRail studied 1.1 million small business conversations and found that up to
85% of callers who reach voicemail never call back. They do not leave a
message. They do not try again tomorrow. They call the next business on the
list.

So a missed call is not a delayed conversation. Most of the time, it is a
lost customer.

## The math for a home services business

Say you run [plumbing](/plumbing) or [HVAC](/hvac). Your average ticket is around $400, and you
close about 40% of the inquiries that reach you.

Expected value of one answered call: $400 x 40% = $160.

Miss five calls in a week, which is easy during a busy day or after hours,
and that is $800 walking past your business. Over a month, roughly $3,200.
Over a year, you do not want to know. (It is about $38,000.)

## The math for a dental practice

A new patient is worth far more than one visit. Between the first appointment,
cleanings, and the occasional crown, lifetime value often lands between
$1,500 and $3,000.

One missed new-patient call a week at a 30% close rate: about $450 to $900 of
expected value, every single week, from a phone that rang while nobody was
free to pick it up.

## The math for a restaurant

A table for four on a Friday night is maybe $120 in revenue. Miss three
reservation calls on a busy weekend and you have handed $360 to the place
down the street. Restaurants feel this one the fastest because the loss shows
up the same night.

## Why this keeps happening

It is not a staffing problem. It is a physics problem. Your best employee can
only answer one call at a time, and calls do not politely wait their turn.
They come during the lunch rush, during the job, at 7pm when you are finally
sitting down.

An AI receptionist answers every call in about four seconds, on your existing
business number. It books the appointment, takes the order, or captures the
callback with the caller's name, number, and reason. The urgent stuff gets
flagged to you instantly. Nothing sits in a voicemail box.
